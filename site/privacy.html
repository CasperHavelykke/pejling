Privacy Policy

Effective 29 September 2026 · Dansk

Pejling collects no information about you. Everything you enter stays on your own phone and is never sent anywhere.

Who is behind the app

Pejling is developed by Casper Havelykke Larsen, Denmark. If you have questions, write to casper.roket@proton.me.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your weight and sex, as entered by you. They are used for the calculation.
  • Your entries: what you tapped, and when.
  • Your choice between the simple and the advanced view.

This information is kept in the app's own storage on the phone. I have no access to it and cannot see it.

What the app does not do

  • It requires no account and no login.
  • It sends no data to any server.
  • It uses no analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • It shares nothing with anyone.
  • It does not ask for access to location, contacts, camera or anything else.

Backups on your phone

If you have enabled backups with Apple or Google, the app's data may be included in your own backup. That backup is covered by Apple's or Google's terms, and I have no access to it.

How to delete your data

You can remove a single entry with "Fortryd" (undo) in the list. Deleting the app deletes all your entries and settings with it.

App Store and Google Play

Apple and Google may collect information when you download and use apps, such as crash reports if you have agreed to share them. This happens under their own privacy policies and not through Pejling.

This website

The website uses no cookies and no tracking, and the server keeps no log of who visits it.

Age

Pejling is for adults only and is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

If the app changes so that this policy no longer fits, this page will be updated and the date at the top will be changed.
