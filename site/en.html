Pejling Dansk

Keep an eye on the night

Tap every time you have a drink. Pejling shows how many drinks are still active in your body, and when it is time for a break.

Get it on the App Store

  • One tap per beer, wine, cocktail or shot.
  • The number drops by itself as your body burns off the alcohol.
  • A calendar shows your earlier nights.
  • No account. Everything stays on your own phone.

Pejling is a guide only. It does not measure anything and cannot be used to judge whether you may drive. The app is for adults only.
